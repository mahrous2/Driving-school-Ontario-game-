How you are marked

  • Minor errors cost 2–5 points (e.g. no signal, no shoulder check, stopped over the line).
  • Major errors cost 10–15 points (rolling stop, unsafe lane change, failing to yield).
  • Dangerous actions cost 25+ points and are an automatic fail in Mock Test mode (red light, passing a school bus, failing to yield to a pedestrian, collisions…).
  • Pass: score ≥ 80 with no dangerous actions.

Key Ontario habits

  • Before every lane change, merge or pull-out: mirror (X) → signal → shoulder check (Z/C) → move.
  • All-way stop: first to stop goes first; if tied, yield to the vehicle on your right.
  • Right on red: full stop behind the line (count 3 s), yield to pedestrians & traffic, obey "No Right Turn on Red" signs.
  • School bus red lights: stop in BOTH directions unless a median divides the road; stop 20 m behind.
  • Streetcar doors open: stop 2 m behind the rear doors until passengers reach the curb.
  • Emergency vehicle: pull to the right and stop. Stopped on the shoulder? Slow down and move over.
  • Following distance: 3 seconds minimum, double it on snow and ice.
